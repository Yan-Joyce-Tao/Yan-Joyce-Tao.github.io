## About Me

Hello! I am a Ph.D. student in [Information Science](https://infosci.cornell.edu/) at Cornell University, working with [Prof. René F. Kizilcec](https://rene.kizilcec.com/) in the [Future of Learning Lab](https://learning.cis.cornell.edu/). 
I study the use of AI in education, with a particular focus on educators' perspectives. My research explores how teachers understand AI — what they believe it is, how they perceive its affordances, to what extent they trust AI, and how they view their relationship with AI in teaching. I also design interventions to support teachers in integrating AI effectively and ethically into their practice.

Before coming to Cornell, I earned my Master of Science in Education from the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/), where I had the honor of being advised by [Prof. Ryan S. Baker](https://learninganalytics.upenn.edu/ryanbaker/).
